Panela

A group expense-splitting app that helps people plan before they spend, a step Splitwise and Tricount leave out. It has budgets by spending category, cost splitting, settlement through Pix, the Brazilian instant payment system, and an AI that sums up where the group stands.

  • Four ways to split: equally, by percentage, by fixed amount or payer only. Settlement suggests the fewest possible transfers.
  • Planning with budgets and reserves by spending category, with push alerts at 80% of the budget and when it goes past 100%.
  • AI to suggest budget items, read receipts and summarize the group in plain language.
  • Automated functional API tests: 20 of 20 cases and 90 checks, including the permission rules.

3Summa

A product for accounting firms that I'm building with my two partners on the Nexa Solution team. It's built around client service over WhatsApp, with a shared inbox for the team and real-time cost tracking in Brazilian reais. Within the team, my focus is the front end.

  • Incoming messages arrive by webhook with HMAC-SHA256 signature validation and idempotent writes, so the same event is never processed twice.
  • Cost dashboard in reais, with configurable rates and an automatically updated dollar exchange rate.
  • Agent accounts with Argon2id, revocable sessions, access roles and email-based 2FA.
  • Per-client data isolation in PostgreSQL is on the roadmap, on the way to becoming SaaS.

Agribusiness SaaS

A management system for the agribusiness chain in Rio Grande do Sul, from farmer to cooperative. What sets it apart is an AI agent on WhatsApp that looks up and records data by message.

  • LangChain agent using Llama 3.3 70B (Groq) and Claude, with tools for inventory, batches, weather and semantic search.
  • Semantic search with pgvector and embeddings, in the same PostgreSQL as the business data.
  • Batch inventory with FEFO and alerts, QR code traceability, and compliance with LGPD, the Brazilian data protection law, through versioned consents.
  • Incremental back-end migration from Django to Rust, keeping the same HTTP contract.

Up Espaço

A platform that connects the staff of an early childhood development center with families: attendance, an activity feed with photos, a daily timeline for each child, developmental milestones, a calendar and announcements.

BookGuess

A literary Wordle. Every day there's a new excerpt from a classic book; players try to guess the book or the author and can ask for an AI-generated hint, which costs half their points.

  • RAG hints: the API finds similar excerpts in pgvector and passes that context to Claude.
  • Answers are checked on the server with SSR, so the title never reaches the browser too early.
  • Real-time leaderboard with Redis Sorted Sets and a cached daily challenge.
  • Public domain works only. One version in .NET with Clean Architecture and another in Next.js + FastAPI, deployed with Docker, Nginx and SSL.
